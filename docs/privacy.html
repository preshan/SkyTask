SkyTask Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

SkyTask (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is developed by Preshan Pradeepa Kariyawasam. This Privacy Policy explains what information SkyTask processes, why, and your choices. It is intended to meet Google Play disclosure expectations.

1. Summary

SkyTask is local-first. Tasks, reminders, ideas, notes, and voice memos are stored on your device by default.

Optional features (calendar sync, Google Sign-In / Firebase, cloud messaging, backups you export) process additional data only when you use them.

We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information the app processes

  • App content you create: titles, descriptions, categories, due dates/times, completion status, and similar fields.
  • Voice memos: audio files you record, stored in app private storage on your device.
  • Reminders & notifications: reminder times and notification settings so alerts can fire on time.
  • Calendar data (optional): if you enable calendar sync, the app reads and writes calendar events on your device calendars (including Google Calendar when that account is available).
  • Security: PIN / biometric unlock preferences for app lock and private items (credentials stay on device).
  • Backups (optional): if you export a backup, a file you choose may include your app content.
  • Account / Firebase (optional): if you sign in, identifiers such as account email and auth tokens may be processed by Firebase Auth / Google Sign-In / Firestore / FCM as needed for that feature.
  • Diagnostics: standard Android / Play crash or performance signals may be processed by the platform; the app does not currently include a separate third-party analytics SDK.

3. Permissions and why we ask

  • Microphone: record voice memos attached to tasks, reminders, ideas, or notes.
  • Notifications: show reminder alerts.
  • Exact alarms: fire reminders at the time you chose, including after reboot where allowed.
  • Calendar (read & write): optional sync of reminders to a device / Google calendar you select.
  • Biometric / fingerprint: optional app unlock.
  • Storage (legacy Android versions only): limited access to pick/save backup files where needed.
  • Internet: optional cloud / auth / messaging features and opening links.

4. How we use information

We use data only to provide SkyTask features you request: organizing content, reminding you on time, syncing to a calendar you enable, securing private content, and restoring backups you import.

We do not use your content for advertising profiling today.

5. Advertising (current and future)

SkyTask does not show ads in the current release.

Future versions may include ads (for example Google AdMob). If ads are added, we will update this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data safety / App content answers before or when that version ships, and disclose any additional data those ad SDKs collect.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your data.

Data may be processed by service providers only when you use related features, for example Google / Android calendar provider, Google Sign-In / Firebase, and Google Play services on the device.

You may also share backups or content yourself through the Android share sheet or files you export.

7. Retention and deletion

Content remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Exported backup files you saved elsewhere are under your control.

8. Children’s privacy

SkyTask is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

We use platform security features such as app-private storage, optional PIN / biometric lock, and optional encryption for private content. No method is 100% secure.

10. Your choices

  • Deny microphone, calendar, or notification permission in system Settings.
  • Turn calendar sync off in SkyTask Settings.
  • Turn app lock off after verifying your identity.
  • Export or delete your data from within the app.
  • Contact us with privacy questions.
