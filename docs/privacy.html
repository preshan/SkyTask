SkyTask Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

SkyTask is built by Preshan Pradeepa Kariyawasam. This page describes what the app stores, what permissions it uses, and how you stay in control.

1. The short version

SkyTask keeps your tasks, reminders, ideas, notes, and voice memos on your phone by default.

Optional features such as calendar sync, Google Sign-In, Firebase, or a backup you export only process extra data when you use them.

We do not sell your personal information. SkyTask does not show ads.

2. What the app stores

  • Content you create - titles, notes, categories, due dates and times, completion status, and similar fields
  • Voice memos - recordings you make, kept in the app's private storage
  • Reminders - times and notification settings so alerts can fire when you asked
  • Calendar - only if you turn on calendar sync; the app can read and write events on calendars you choose
  • App lock - PIN or biometric preferences stay on the device
  • Backups - only if you export one; the file you save is under your control
  • Account - only if you sign in; Google / Firebase may handle email and auth tokens for that feature
  • Diagnostics - Android or Play may record crash or performance signals; SkyTask does not add a separate analytics SDK

3. Permissions

  • Microphone - voice memos on tasks, reminders, ideas, or notes
  • Notifications - reminder alerts
  • Exact alarms - reminders at the time you set, including after reboot when the system allows it
  • Calendar - optional sync to a device or Google calendar you select
  • Biometrics - optional unlock
  • Storage - only on older Android versions when picking or saving backup files
  • Internet - optional cloud or sign-in features, and opening links

4. How data is used

Data is used to run the features you ask for: organizing your content, sending reminders on time, syncing a calendar you enable, locking private items, and restoring backups you import.

Your content is not used for advertising.

5. Ads

SkyTask does not contain ads.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your data.

When you use related features, data may pass through providers such as the device calendar, Google Sign-In / Firebase, or Google Play services.

You can also share backups or content yourself with the Android share sheet or files you export.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Content stays on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall SkyTask.

Deleting an item removes it from local storage. Removing a voice memo deletes that recording.

Uninstalling removes app-private data on the phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not removed automatically.

8. Children

SkyTask is not aimed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

SkyTask uses app-private storage, optional PIN or biometric lock, and optional encryption for private items.

10. Your choices

  • Turn off microphone, calendar, or notification access in system Settings
  • Turn calendar sync off in SkyTask Settings
  • Turn app lock off after verifying it's you
  • Export or delete your data in the app
  • Email us with questions
